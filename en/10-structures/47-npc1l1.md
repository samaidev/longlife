## 1. Why It Matters: NPC1L1 Is the Intestinal Gate of "Cholesterol Absorption"

NPC1L1 is the rate-limiting transporter of "intestinal cholesterol absorption" — the core "valve" at the "entrance" of the "cholesterol economy":

- **"'Entrance' of 'cholesterol'"**: Dietary cholesterol → intestinal brush border → NPC1L1 uptake (endocytosis) → enters enterocytes → chylomicron assembly → into blood — "'Gate' at the 'entrance' (NPC1L1) → cholesterol 'enters' (intestine→blood) — the 'main force' of 'absorption'";
- **"'Target' of 'ezetimibe'"**: Ezetimibe (marketed in 2002) inhibits NPC1L1 → intestinal cholesterol absorption ↓~50% → plasma LDL ↓ (15–20% as monotherapy, stronger combined with statins) — "Block the 'entrance' (ezetimibe inhibits NPC1L1) → cholesterol 'enters less' → LDL 'drops' — a 'classic' 'absorption' target";
- **"'Closed loop' of the 'three valves'"**: Absorption (NPC1L1) + synthesis (HMGCR, [45-hmgcr.md](45-hmgcr.md)) + clearance (PCSK9, [46-pcsk9.md](46-pcsk9.md)) → three valves of the "cholesterol economy" → combined intervention is "strongest" — "'Three valves' controlled together' (absorption↓ + synthesis↓ + clearance↑) → cholesterol 'extremely' reduced — 'complete' management of the 'economy'";
- **"'Genetic' 'evidence'"**: NPC1L1 loss-of-function mutations → cholesterol absorption ↓ → LDL ↓ + cardiovascular events ↓ (natural experiment) → target validation — "Gene 'mutation' (NPC1L1 loss) → absorption 'naturally' low + LDL 'low' + cardioprotective' (natural experiment) — 'strong' evidence for the 'target'";
- **"'Dietary' 'interaction'"**: Phytosterols (stanols) → compete for NPC1L1 → inhibit cholesterol absorption ("natural ezetimibe") → dietary "adjunct" — "Plant 'sterols' 'grab the entrance' (compete for NPC1L1) → cholesterol 'enters less' — 'naturally' 'regulating' 'absorption'";

**Structure Overview (6V3H, NPC1L1 + ezetimibe analog)**: NPC1L1 is a "multi-pass transmembrane protein" (~13 transmembrane passes, containing N-terminal MLD domain / central CTD domain / transmembrane domain TMD) — "three 'large domains': MLD (N-terminal, cholesterol binding) + CTD (central, conserved domain) + TMD (transmembrane, transport channel) — ezetimibe 'binds' in the 'transmembrane domain' (central 'cavity' of TMD) → 'blocks' the cholesterol 'channel' — the 'blueprint' of the 'absorption' gate is clear (2020 cryo-EM)".

## 2. Structural Anatomy: What the "Intestinal Gate" Looks Like in 6V3H
