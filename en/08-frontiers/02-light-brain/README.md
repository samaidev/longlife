# Topic 2 · Light, Brain, and Rejuvenation

[English index](../../README.md) · [Chinese source index](../../../08-frontiers/02-light-brain/README.md)

## Status statements in the Chinese source

The statements below translate claims in the source index. They are not independently verified and may be time-sensitive:

- The source claims evidence for a light–CCO–NO–ATP segment and reports an ATP increase of 35–40%; this figure requires verification in the cited studies and depends on experimental context.
- The source states that more than 99% of light is attenuated before reaching the hypothalamus and proposes focused ultrasound as a targeting approach; verify this technical claim in the primary literature.
- The proposed light/acoustic targeting → hypothalamic SIRT1 → systemic rejuvenation chain is a hypothesis, not an established clinical outcome.
