## Research priorities as stated in the Chinese source

The source gives a tiered list based on its assessment of evidence maturity, including examples it labels as clinical/human, translational animal research, and early exploration. These rankings and status claims are time-sensitive and have not been independently verified in this translation; consult the cited primary sources and current trial registries before relying on them.

## Common research framework

The source says each review follows: **mechanism → technical approach → evidence level → bottlenecks → experimental design → roadmap**.
